01 / Challenge

The old game is going away.

For years, scarce development capacity shaped product organisations: the planning, the roles, the way decisions were made. AI is making building faster and cheaper. Your competitors get those capabilities too.

What companies try first
  • Someone gets a title for doing "a lot with AI".
  • New tools are bought to solve it.
  • Everyone gets access to Claude.
  • There are inspiration sessions and hackathons.
  • A Slack channel shares the latest AI news.
  • Only a small group are early adopters.

A bigger backlog delivered faster is no guarantee of a better business. What matters is which problems you solve, how well you solve them, and how quickly your organisation learns and acts. The business you have built is the starting point. Staying competitive means examining which choices, skills and ways of working will serve it next.

02 / Consequences

The price of the old game.

Features go live, meetings fill the week and everyone works hard. Yet the results you need keep waiting.

Cost / 01

Work that ships without the intended result.

The feature works as specified. The customer problem remains. The investment still needs maintenance and attention.

shipped, no result
Cost / 02

Time lost before anything improves.

Decisions wait. Work comes back for clarification. Dependencies hold teams up. Feedback arrives after the next commitment has already been made.

time lost
Cost / 03

Answers paid for twice.

A question your company investigated before gets researched again. The evidence exists, but nobody uses it.

paid twice
Cost / 04

Opportunities that never get their turn.

Capacity stays committed. Important improvements wait. Another quarter passes before the business can act.

never its turn

These costs differ by company. We establish where they sit in yours and which improvement is worth pursuing first.

03 / Approach

How to get to the new game.

We work with you and your teams on the result you need. We establish what is holding progress back, agree what needs to change and help make it happen.

That includes the skills, tools and decisions the work demands. We combine training, coaching and hands-on execution where they are needed. Existing strengths, people and systems are our starting point.

The Learn Faster To Win Loop keeps the whole picture in view.

04 / Companies

Choosing here is not losing.

Our focus is ambitious product leaders at established, tech-enabled businesses.

A good fitFit
  • You have customers, a working product or service and ambitions for what comes next.
  • Product development is a recurring investment in the business.
  • You own the product decisions, whether development happens internally or with partners.
  • A leader has the authority to act, and the people involved can participate.
  • You want the work to produce results you can point to.
Outside our focusNo fit
  • Businesses still searching for their first product and customers.
  • Assignments whose main purpose is filling a temporary product vacancy.
  • Engagements without access to the people, information or decisions needed to achieve the outcome.
  • Requests to endorse a fixed solution without examining whether it addresses the problem.
05 / Outcomes

Results you can point to.

We agree which results the engagement must deliver and what evidence will show progress.

Outcome

An outcome that improved.

A customer problem reduced, service costs lowered, retention improved or another result defined in your terms.

Time

Less time lost.

Fewer avoidable waits between identifying a worthwhile problem and making a useful improvement.

Teams

Teams that can act.

People understand the direction, bring forward proposals and take decisions within clear responsibilities.

Investment

Investment that responds to evidence.

What works receives further commitment. What falls short gets changed or stopped.

Capability

Capability that stays.

We assess the result against the commitment we made together. Where benefits take longer to appear, we agree who follows up and when.
