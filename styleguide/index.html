Learn Faster to Win helps product leaders at scale-ups build what they should. The company that learns fastest wins, so the brand borrows its world from the one place where learning speed is measured to the thousandth: the pit wall of a racing team.

Every lap is a loop. The pit wall decides.

Most consultancies look like the boardroom they sell into. This one looks like the timing screen. Speed with control: sectors, telemetry, a verdict at every pit stop, and a car that comes back a little better each lap. Someone who has never watched a race still reads it as fast and precise. That is the test.

06 / Motion

Timing-screen motion. Values arrive, then settle.

  • Sector bars fill left to right, 240ms, linear.
  • Counters count like lap timers, tabular, 600ms at most.
  • Reveals: 200ms opacity plus an 8px rise, sharp ease-out.
  • One purple sweep across the top of the page on load.
  • No bounce, no glow pulses, no parallax, no scroll-jacking. Reduced motion turns everything off.

07 / Do and don't

The metaphor is structural, not a pun.

Do

  • Use lap, sector, pit wall, pit stop, telemetry, delta as fixed vocabulary.
  • Show real numbers in deltas and towers, or show none.
  • Alternate Carbon, Pit White and Asphalt; neighbours never share a tone.
  • Keep headlines literal: "The fastest learner wins."
  • Photograph Stefan at work, graded to Carbon, purple only in overlays.

Don't

  • No cars, helmets, tyres, chequered flags, podiums.
  • No team colours, circuit names, "F1" or "Formula" anywhere.
  • No pole position, full throttle, fast lane, race ahead in copy.
  • No signal colour outside a verdict, delta or status.
  • No skew, glow, gradient or motion blur. That was v1.

01 / Purple stays or goes

The case for staying: on the timing screen purple means "faster than everyone", and no racing team owns it. If the colour itself was the problem with v1, the system holds with another accent and nothing else changes.

02 / How far the metaphor goes in copy

Proposal: six fixed terms (lap, sector, pit wall, pit stop, telemetry, delta) and nothing more. Headlines stay literal.

03 / Barlow Condensed as the face

If the condensed pit-board feel reads too sporty, the alternative is Archivo Expanded for display: wider, more engineering, less pit board. Body and telemetry stay.
