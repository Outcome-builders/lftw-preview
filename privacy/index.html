2 / What we collect, and why

When you request something from us

The book preview, the masterclass, the free skills and similar material each ask for a first name and an e-mail address. We use them to send you what you asked for, and the short series of e-mails that follows. Every e-mail carries a one-click unsubscribe. Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You may withdraw it at any moment, and withdrawal does not affect what happened before it.

When you take the route check

The route check on this site runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us and not stored by us. If you go on to plan a call, the route that came out and your role are passed along with the booking, so Stefan knows what to prepare.

When you e-mail us or book a call

Your name, e-mail address, whatever you write to us, and the time of the call. We use it to answer you and to have the conversation. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, and, once we are talking about work, the steps taken before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

When we work together

Business contact details of the people involved, and whatever you share with us in the course of the engagement. We only use it to deliver the work and to invoice it. Legal basis: performance of the contract, and our legal obligation to keep an administration (Article 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR).

When you simply visit the website

Our host records standard server logs, including your IP address, briefly, in order to deliver the page and to defend against abuse. We run no analytics on this site at the moment, and the site sets no tracking cookies; the cookie statement explains what the site does and does not store. If we add a cookieless tool, this page names it. Legal basis, according to Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest in a website that works.

3 / Who else sees your data

We use a small number of service providers. Each of them processes data on our instructions under a data processing agreement, and none of them may use it for their own purposes.

  • Cloudflare: hosting this website. United States and the European Union, under Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Cal.com: booking calls. You leave this site to book, so your browser connects to Cal.com when you do. European Union and United States, under Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • E-mail platform (to be confirmed): sending the e-mail series. United States, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

The typefaces on this site are served from our own host, so no font provider sees your visit.

We do not sell your data, we do not rent it, and we do not trade it. We hand it to authorities only where the law leaves us no choice.

4 / How long we keep it

  • E-mail subscription: until you unsubscribe, then removed within 30 days.
  • E-mail correspondence: two years after the last message.
  • Client records and invoices: seven years, because Dutch tax law requires it.
  • Server logs: as long as our host keeps them, which is a matter of days.

5 / Your rights

The GDPR gives you a set of rights, and we will honour every one of them without making you work for it.

  • Access. Ask what we hold about you, and we will tell you.
  • Rectification. Something wrong? We correct it.
  • Erasure. Ask us to delete your data and we will, unless the law requires us to keep it, which in practice only applies to invoices.
  • Restriction and objection. You can object to processing based on legitimate interest, and you can ask us to pause processing while a dispute is resolved.
  • Portability. Ask for your data in a machine-readable file, and you will get it.
  • Withdraw consent. One click in any e-mail, or one line to us.

Mail stefan@learnfastertowin.com. We answer within 30 days, and usually within two working days. There is no charge.

If you are not happy with how we handle it, you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. We would rather you told us first, so we can fix it.

6 / Security

We keep the number of places your data lives as small as we can, we use providers with credible security practices, and we protect our accounts with two-factor authentication. No system is perfect. If a breach ever puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, as the law requires.

7 / Children

This website is aimed at professionals. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe we have, mail us and we will delete it.

8 / Automated decisions

We do not make automated decisions about you, and we do not profile you. The route check gives you a recommendation on the spot, from your own answers, and nothing is recorded.

9 / Changes

When this statement changes, we update the date at the top. If a change is significant and you are on our list, we will tell you by e-mail rather than quietly editing the page. How an engagement itself works, including the data processing agreement we sign for client work, is set out in the terms and conditions.
