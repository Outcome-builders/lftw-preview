Legal / Cookie statement

Cookie statement

We set no tracking cookies, so there is no cookie banner to click away. The site stores nothing on your device. The only exception is booking a call, which happens on Cal.com's own pages.

1 / What we do not do

No advertising cookies. No tracking pixels. No cross-site tracking. No third-party profiling. We do not know who you are when you read this page, and we have not built any way to find out.

Under Dutch law (Article 11.7a of the Telecommunicatiewet) and the GDPR, consent is required for cookies that track you. We do not use any, which is why you are reading this page instead of dismissing a banner.

2 / What the site stores

Nothing. The route check keeps your answers in the page while you take it and forgets them when you leave. There is no theme switch, no login, and no analytics script.

3 / Booking a call

When you plan a call, you leave this site for Cal.com. Cal.com can set cookies on its own pages in order to make the booking work. Those cookies are functional: they exist to let you book, which is the reason you went there. If you would rather not, mail us instead at stefan@learnfastertowin.com and we arrange the call by hand.

What you type into the calendar reaches us through Cal.com as our processor; the privacy statement says what happens to it and how long we keep it.

4 / Who sees your IP address

One party sees your IP address when you load this page: Cloudflare, which hosts the site and needs your address to serve you the page. That is inherent to how the web works. The typefaces are served from our own host, so no font provider is involved.

5 / If we ever change this

If we add anything that tracks you, we will ask for your consent first, in a way you can refuse without losing access to the site. We would rather not add it at all.

6 / Questions

Mail stefan@learnfastertowin.com. See also the privacy statement and the terms and conditions.
