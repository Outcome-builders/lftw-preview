What is in scope, what it costs, and how long it runs is set out in the quotation. The quotation prevails over this page where the two differ.

4 / Quotations and how an engagement starts

Quotations are valid for 30 days unless they say otherwise, and prices are in euros and exclusive of VAT. An engagement exists once you have accepted the quotation in writing, e-mail included, or once we have started work at your request.

5 / Payment

  • If you pay late, statutory commercial interest and reasonable collection costs apply, and we may suspend the work until the invoice is settled.

6 / Cancellation and rescheduling

The Program

  • More than 14 days before the start: full refund, or move to a later cohort at no cost.
  • Within 14 days of the start: move to a later cohort at no cost. No refund.
  • After the Program has started: no refund. The materials remain yours.

If you take part as a consumer rather than through a company, you have a statutory right of withdrawal of 14 days. It lapses once the Program has started, if you have asked us to start within that period.

If too few people enrol, we may cancel a cohort. You then get a full refund or a place in the next one, whichever you prefer.

The Intensive and the Partnership

Either party may end the engagement with 30 days’ written notice. Work already done is invoiced. Sessions cancelled less than 5 working days ahead may be charged in full, because the time was reserved for you and cannot be resold at that notice.
